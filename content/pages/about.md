**Society background**

Welcome to the Trinity College Science Society (TCSS), Cambridge’s most active student scientific society. At our core, the TCSS is dedicated to providing weekly talks from truly eminent academics, hoping to enrich Trinity’s great scientific history. We invite leading researchers to share their insights on a wide range of fields, offering students the chance to engage with cutting-edge research and engage with their interests beyond the course. From Nobel Laureates to directors of world-leading laboratories, this year’s talks will cover a variety of disciplines and levels, all from some of the most influential scientists of this country and beyond.  

Beyond our talks, TCSS hosts a variety of social events such as quizzes, formal swaps, and our popular annual garden party. These gatherings offer a great way to meet fellow enthusiasts and enjoy the vibrant community within Trinity and the whole university, making like-minded friends from across the sciences and at all stages of their courses.  
 
To stay updated on all our events, sign up for our mailing list by emailing hh638@cam.ac.uk, follow us on Instagram @trinscisoc, and make sure to visit us at Chaplain’s Squash, our Freshers’ Week event and more. We will circulate a term card containing the times and dates for our events, which will also be available on our website.  

We are thoroughly excited to have you join us in sharing our passion.
