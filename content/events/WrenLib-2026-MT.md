In collaboration with the Wren librarians, we will be exploring the scientific treasures of the Wren and its archives. With features from the heavy hitters (think Newton, Maxwell, Faraday, Thomson(s), Frisch, Eddington, Chandrasekhar, Adrian, Hodgkin and Huxley) and some more recent lab books, there should be something for everyone - not just the physicists and physiologists!
